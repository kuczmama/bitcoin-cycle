Model assumptions

  • 2025 cycle ATH: approximately $126,223; 2025 year-end anchor: $88,474.
  • Projected ATHs: $166,500 in October 2029 and $189,100 in October 2033.
  • Monthly values are logarithmically interpolated between explicit cycle landmarks, including bear-market lows, recoveries and peaks.
  • The model intentionally assumes diminishing returns and less severe drawdowns over time. It does not estimate probability or react to macroeconomic conditions.

Live data: CoinGecko keyless public API. Important: Bitcoin can materially diverge from historical cycle patterns; this dashboard is for scenario monitoring only.
